Why this matters

Knowing the targets isn't the same as reaching them.

Every Wright provider already knows the targets. The gap isn't knowledge — it's what happens at minute eleven of a busy visit.

You know that hypertension control means under 140/80. You know A1C control means under 9. You know which patients are due for which screening. The clinical knowledge is in place.

What value-based care asks of you is different. It asks that the knowledge becomes reflex — that the wellness conversation happens in the medication-refill visit, in the dizziness visit, in the visit that ran over schedule. Not when there's time. Always.

Reflex is what this training is designed to build.

i
Training that teaches knowledge changes what you know.
Lectures, modules, and reading material build the foundation. Every provider here has that foundation. It is necessary but not sufficient.
ii
Training that teaches reflex changes what you do.
Reflex is built only through repetition under realistic pressure. Reading about a conversation does not change behavior. Practicing the conversation does.
iii
Role-play is how you build the reflex.
You'll practice The Wellness Dialogue with patients who respond, push back, and adapt — exactly as your patients do — until the dialogue becomes the way you conduct any encounter.
"The objective is not providers who recite a structure. It is providers for whom the dialogue is the way they practice."
